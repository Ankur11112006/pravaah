Flood decision system  /  India  /  build report

PRAVAAH

We set out to predict which houses flood. We wrote the test that would kill that claim, ran it on two real floods, and it failed. This is what the failure taught us, what we built instead, and what runs today.

PS3 · Disaster Response Intelligence Platform Patna 2019 · Delhi 2023 · Mahanadi 2025 All sources open, no key, no login
01

The problem, as stated and as it actually is

India loses roughly 1,600 lives and Rs 1,800 crore to floods a year. The brief asks for a platform that turns flood prediction into emergency planning and resource allocation.

The forecasts already exist. IMD runs a 21-member ensemble. CWC operates 354 gauged forecast stations with official danger levels. NDMA's SACHET can reach every handset in the country. What none of them produce is a decision: how many boats, to which ward, along which road, by when, and who cannot be reached at all.

That gap is the problem. But there is a second problem underneath it, and we only found it by testing ourselves.

The claim we started with

Free terrain and satellite data can predict urban flooding at building level, using HAND (Height Above Nearest Drainage).

The test we wrote to kill it

Hindcast two real floods against Sentinel-1 radar. Beat a one-line elevation threshold. Reach recall above 70% at precision above 50%.

Verdict

Failed. HAND did not beat the one-line baseline. Best precision anywhere was 9%, against a 50% target.

02

What the test actually found

Three findings, in rising order of how much they mattered.

The event everyone cites has no ground truth

The design named Cyclone Michaung, Chennai, December 2023. Sentinel-1 passed over Chennai on 6, 18 and 30 November and then not again until 17 January: a 48-day gap containing the entire cyclone. Confirmed against two independent catalogues. Every optical scene in that window is 65 to 99% cloud. So we chose the event by ground-truth availability instead, and of ten major Indian floods only two have radar within three days of peak.

The ground truth is not the disaster

Of 455,860 buildings in Patna, radar labels 659 as flooded. During a week-long urban flood. The flood it does map sits a median of 134 metres from the river in Patna and 170 in Delhi: it is the channel widening, not the city drowning. Water among dense buildings raises radar backscatter through double-bounce against walls instead of lowering it.

Confirmed from the ground. The Bihar Inter Agency Group and Sphere India rapid needs assessment reports 80% of Patna houses took water, and names Rajendra Nagar, Kankarbagh, Boring Road and Pataliputra Colony as worst hit. All are pluvial, kilometres from the Ganga. None of them appear in the satellite flood map.

Consequence

The design's own test cannot be passed by anyone on free radar, because the ground truth does not contain the phenomenon. That is a statement about the field, not about our code.

03

What we built instead

Depth became an input. The system stopped guessing where water goes and started doing the part nobody does. 

HAZARD     a depth map, from CWC's own model or fitted to observation
NETWORK    road graph carrying terrain, not a snapshot
EXPOSURE   who and how many stand in the water, by reporting unit
ROUTES     who can still reach a shelter, and who depends on a bridge
ALLOCATE   min-cost flow to shelters of measured capacity
ALERT      cost-loss trigger, CAP 1.2 XML, 160-character SMS

Where no official depth field exists, the water level is fitted to what the satellite observed at a known discharge, on two dates, then driven forward by the discharge series. That is not prediction from terrain; it is filling a surface whose height was measured.

The check that mattered

CWC's Gandhighat gauge sits 3.8 km from our reference point with an official danger level of 48.60 m. Our independently fitted level for 30 September 2019 was 48.70 m.

Result

Ten centimetres, by a completely separate route. The rapid needs assessment reports "in most of the slums water level was 3 feet" (0.91 m); our modelled median over flooded land is 0.73 m. That agreement got slightly worse once the river channel was excluded from the flood, which is the right direction: the river cells were deep, and counting them flattered the median into a match it had not earned.

04

What makes it different

Not the modelling. Every number below comes from something the system says that other systems do not.

It names who it cannot reach

593 people in Patna have no road route to any shelter at any water level. They are on river islands. A bus is not coming and the plan says so, instead of quietly excluding them from the total.

It refuses to guess about bridges

A terrain model reads the river under a bridge deck, which made every Ganga bridge appear under nine metres of water. Bridges are now neither called open nor flooded. The system reports that 17,225 people are placed only because a bridge is assumed passable.

Roads change mode, they do not vanish

Under 0.3 m a car passes; to 0.5 m a two-wheeler; to 1.5 m a person on foot; above that a boat. Each road carries its own controlling ground height, so a bus and a rescue boat get different maps of the same district, and the bus's map expires first.

Capacity is measured, not assumed

Shelter capacity is the roof footprint inside each campus divided by the NDMA minimum of 3.5 m² per person. Our earlier guesses were wrong in both directions: a school assumed at 500 measured 288; one college assumed at 1,000 measured 10,020.

The trigger is arithmetic, not a hunch

Act when probability exceeds cost divided by loss. The probability is computed live as the share of a 50-member forecast ensemble above a published return period. Nobody types it.

It stops rather than answer wrongly

A calibration that misses observed extent by more than 15% is rejected and the run halts. A discharge that disagrees with the river's own return periods by an order of magnitude is refused. A missing population tile raises instead of reporting nobody.

Running on a government operational forecast

C-Flood, run by C-DAC with NRSC and CWC under the National Supercomputing Mission, publishes a hydrodynamic inundation forecast: 30 m depth in metres, every three hours out to 48, with 173 archived runs. We pull it directly. For the Mahanadi flood of 30 July 2025, at 1.83 times the two-year return period, the system reports 510 km² wet and 178,411 people straight off CWC's own model.

Backend

FastAPI over SQLite, 44 of 44 tests passing. Read endpoints serve what the pipeline computed. Write endpoints accept the three things no instrument can see.

Phone numbers are stored hashed: the system needs to recognise a repeat caller, not hold a list of flood victims' numbers.

06

What does not work, and why

Stated plainly, because a system that hides its gaps is the one that fails in the field.

GapStatusWhat it would take
Urban pluvial floodingunsolved No dataset sees water in a dense Indian city. Rajendra Nagar and Kankarbagh appear in no gauge, no radar mask and no model run. This is India's largest flood problem and the data does not exist.
Allocation in the Mahanadi deltablocked OSM holds 2 shelter polygons for the whole area. Odisha's roughly 880 cyclone shelters are the real list and none are mapped.
Delhi over timepartial Its Yamuna is embanked: connected area jumps from 0 to 121 km² across ten centimetres of level, so no level reproduces the observed extent. It runs as one snapshot and says so.
The plan layer nationallypartial The watch layer covers all 354 stations. The plan layer runs per area, on demand, and needs that area's roads and buildings first.
Citizen page writesnow possible The backend accepts reports, help requests and decisions. Wiring the page to it is a frontend task.

Useful lead time, measured rather than promised

The brief asked for 48 to 72 hours. Using Google's reforecast archive, for Patna 2019 the two-year threshold was crossed on 30 September and the first forecast to call it was issued on 24 September: six days. That is the river signal, which sets the ceiling. Everything downstream spends that time; none of it adds any.
