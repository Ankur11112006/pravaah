Known limits

      

        The water level is fitted to two satellite observations and driven by GloFAS
        discharge. Outside that discharge range the level is an extrapolation and the
        page says so on the slider readout.


        A bridge deck is invisible to a terrain model. Bridges are neither called open
        nor called flooded; the count of people who depend on one is shown instead.


        Shelter capacity is measured, not assumed: the roof footprint inside each
        campus divided by the NDMA minimum of 3.5 sq m per person. It is still an
        estimate. A single-storey roof understates a multi-storey school, and not
        every roofed square metre is usable floor, so the two errors push opposite
        ways and neither is quantified here.
