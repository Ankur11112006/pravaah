What this is
Live water level and CWC's own issued forecast, pulled straight from ffs.india-water.gov.in. No key and no login: the service answers a plain HTTP query. Levels are metres above the station datum and are only comparable to that station's own danger level, never between stations. Reservoir inflow stations are in cumecs and are drawn as squares. Alerts come from NDMA SACHET and the inundation run from C-Flood (inf.cwc.gov.in). All three sources are public and unauthenticated.
