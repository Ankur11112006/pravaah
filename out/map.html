What this is
Water level comes from GloFAS river discharge, fitted to the flood extent Sentinel‑1 actually observed on two dates. The terrain is not being asked to predict where water goes, only to be filled to a level that was measured. Roads carry their own controlling ground height, so their mode is computed live rather than read from stored frames.
